Last updated: November 14, 2026  |  Effective: November 14, 2026

Information a parent enters to personalize a story is processed as described below. We recommend using the app under the supervision of a parent or guardian.

2. Data We Process

2.1. Local Story Data

Created stories, favorite stories and in-app settings are stored only on your device in a local Room database. This data cannot be accessed remotely and is not uploaded to the cloud.

2.2. AI API Use (Story Creation)

A story request is sent to the Google Gemini API through the developer's server, which runs on Google Apps Script. The following information is sent for this purpose:

  • The selected theme, age group, story length, story language and story mode;
  • The character name and any optional names of people, pets or toys you ask to include in the story;
  • Anything you optionally enter in Parental Controls: a behavior note, words to avoid, a toy description and the hero's look in illustrations;
  • When you continue a story: the previous story's title, a short summary of it and the choice the child made.

This information is used only to generate the requested story and is not stored on our server. Data sent to Google is subject to Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

2.2.1. Device ID and Daily Quota

To enforce the daily story limit, your device's app-specific Android ID is sent to our server with each story request. The server keeps only a daily usage count linked to this ID; the counter is deleted automatically the following day. The device ID is not sent to the Gemini API or to the image service, and is not used for advertising, profiling or tracking.

2.3. Images

The story's cover image, page images and coloring page are generated through the Pollinations.ai service. Only an AI-written English description of the scene to draw and the story theme are sent to this service. The child's name is removed from these descriptions and is not sent to the image service.

If you filled in the optional "hero's look in illustrations" field in Parental Controls (for example hair color, glasses, clothing), that description is added to the scene description so the illustrations stay consistent. The app does not ask for or upload photos.

Images are downloaded to your device and cached there. As with any internet connection, Google and Pollinations.ai servers technically see the IP address the request comes from.

2.3.1. QR Codes

QR codes used for sharing stories are generated entirely on your device. No story text, character name or any other data is sent to any server to create them.

2.4. Notifications

Bedtime reminder notifications are scheduled locally on the device. No remote push service such as Firebase is used.

2.5. Accounts and Identity

Dream Tales does not require an account. No email address, full name or identity information is requested for an account.

3. Children's Privacy

The app is designed for children and does not knowingly collect personal data from users under 13. When creating stories, parents or guardians can use a nickname instead of the child's real name or leave the name field empty, and should avoid entering other personal information about the child. Any name or information entered is sent to the Gemini API to generate the story text; the child's name is not sent to the image service.

4. Data Sharing

Your personal data is never shared, sold or rented to third parties for commercial purposes. External services are used only for content generation and subscription management. The app does not show ads.

5. Subscriptions and Free Use

Dream Tales uses an ad-free freemium model: a free tier and a Premium subscription.

  • Every user gets 2 free stories per day.
  • Premium unlocks 10 stories a day, Special Day Stories, Coloring Pages, read-aloud (TTS), story continuation and all other features.
  • Premium plans: monthly and yearly subscriptions (auto-renewing).
  • Subscriptions are managed by Google Play. Purchases, cancellations and refunds are subject to Google Play's billing policies.
  • Manage your subscription at: play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions

The app shows no ads and uses no ad SDK, advertising ID (AAID) or tracking technology. Payments are processed only through Google Play Billing; the developer cannot access your payment information.

6. Data Security and Handling of Personal/Sensitive Data

Dream Tales handles personal data (e.g. a child's name) and potentially sensitive data (e.g. behavioral notes entered by a parent) through the following safeguards:

  • Transport security: All data sent from your device to our server and AI services is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. Cleartext connections are not allowed.
  • Purpose limitation: Personal/sensitive data is processed only to generate the requested story; it is never used for advertising, profiling or sale.
  • Third-party processor: Data is sent to the Google Gemini API to generate stories and is subject to Google's Gemini API terms (ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms). The app uses Gemini as a paid service; under those terms, content submitted through paid use is not used to improve Google's products and may be kept for a limited time to detect abuse. Transfers abroad happen only with your explicit consent.
  • Local storage security: Data on the device is kept in Android's protected, app-private (sandboxed) storage; other apps cannot access it.
  • Data minimization: The app asks only for the minimum information needed to personalize a story; parents can leave the name empty and use the "Random" option without entering any personal data.
  • Server-side access restriction: Our own server (Google Apps Script) keeps only a daily usage counter linked to a device ID; story content and the information you enter are not stored. The counter is deleted automatically the following day, and only the developer can access it.

7. Deleting Data

Stories can be deleted one by one inside the app. To delete all data, go to Settings → Apps → Dream Tales → Clear data. The daily usage counter on the server deletes itself the following day; if you want it removed sooner, write to mecinar27@gmail.com.

Questions: mecinar27@gmail.com

Technologies Used

Dream Tales does not use any SDK for analytics tracking, advertising or user profiling. No ads are shown and no advertising ID (AAID) is collected. Only the following external services are used, for content generation and subscription management.

Used to generate story text. The story information listed in section 2.2 of the Privacy Policy is sent to Google through our server (Google Apps Script). The device ID is not sent to the Gemini API.
