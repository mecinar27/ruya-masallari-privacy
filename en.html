Last updated: September 14, 2026  |  Effective: September 14, 2026
🧒 Important: Dream Tales is an app for children. The app does not collect personal data from children. We recommend using it under the supervision of a parent or guardian.

Dream Tales (Rüya Masalları) is provided by the independent developer mecinar27.

Contact: Email: mecinar27@gmail.com

2. Data We Process

2.1. Local Story Data

Created stories, favorite stories and in-app settings are stored only on your device in a local Room database. This data cannot be accessed remotely and is not uploaded to the cloud.

2.2. AI API Use (Story Creation)

Story creation uses the Google Gemini API. The theme, age group and character name selected by the parent/user, along with any optional names of people, pets or toys to include in the story, are sent to Google servers to generate the story. This data is subject to Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

2.3. Images

Story cover images are generated through the Pollinations.ai service. Only the story theme is sent to this service. No personal information is sent.

2.4. Notifications

Bedtime reminder notifications are scheduled locally on the device. No remote push service such as Firebase is used.

2.5. Accounts and Identity

Dream Tales does not require an account. No email address, name or identity information is collected.

3. Children's Privacy

The app is designed for children and does not knowingly collect personal data from users under 13. Parents or guardians should avoid entering a child's real name or personal information when creating stories. If such information is entered, it will be sent to the Gemini API.

4. Data Sharing

Your personal data is never shared, sold or rented to third parties for commercial purposes. External services are used only for content generation and subscription management. The app does not show ads.

5. Subscriptions and Free Use

Dream Tales uses an ad-free freemium model: a free tier and a Premium subscription.

  • Every user gets 2 free stories per day.
  • Premium unlocks 10 stories a day, Special Day Stories, Coloring Pages, read-aloud (TTS), story continuation and all other features.
  • Premium plans: monthly and yearly subscriptions (auto-renewing).
  • Subscriptions are managed by Google Play. Purchases, cancellations and refunds are subject to Google Play's billing policies.
  • Manage your subscription at: play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions

The app shows no ads and uses no ad SDK, advertising ID (AAID) or tracking technology. Payments are processed only through Google Play Billing; the developer cannot access your payment information.

6. Data Security and Handling of Personal/Sensitive Data

Dream Tales handles personal data (e.g. a child's name) and potentially sensitive data (e.g. behavioral notes entered by a parent) through the following safeguards:

  • Transport security: All data sent from your device to our server and AI services is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. Cleartext connections are not allowed.
  • Purpose limitation: Personal/sensitive data is processed only to generate the requested story; it is never used for advertising, profiling or sale.
  • Third-party processor: Data is sent to the Google Gemini API to generate stories. Under Google's standard API terms, submitted content may be used by Google to improve its products. Details: ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms
  • Local storage security: Data on the device is kept in Android's protected, app-private (sandboxed) storage; other apps cannot access it.
  • Data minimization: The app asks only for the minimum information needed to personalize a story; parents can leave the name empty and use the "Random" option without entering any personal data.
  • Server-side access restriction: Our own server (Google Apps Script) keeps only a daily usage counter linked to a device ID; only the developer can access it, and it is deleted within 30 days on request.

7. Deleting Data

Stories can be deleted one by one inside the app. To delete all data, go to Settings → Apps → Dream Tales → Clear data.

📡 Tracking Technologies Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Technologies Used

Dream Tales does not use any SDK for analytics tracking, advertising or user profiling. No ads are shown and no advertising ID (AAID) is collected. Only the following external services are used, for content generation and subscription management.

External Service 1: Google Gemini API

Used to generate story text. The selected theme, age group and character name are sent to Google servers. No personal identity information is sent.

Used to generate story cover images. The story theme is sent to this service; no personal information is sent.

Used for Premium subscription (monthly/yearly, auto-renewing) purchases. Payment information is processed only by Google; the developer cannot access it. Manage your subscription at: play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions

All story content and app settings are stored locally in an Android Room database. No cloud backup is made.

Notifications

Bedtime reminders are scheduled as local notifications. Firebase or other remote push services are not used.

Controls and Preferences
